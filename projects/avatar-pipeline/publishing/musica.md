## Prompt de referencia

Instrumental synth-pop with a sparkling 1980s-inspired production. Warm analog synthesizers, shimmering arpeggios, clean electric guitar accents, rounded bass and crisp restrained electronic drums. Bright, nostalgic, hopeful and subtly cinematic. 108 BPM, steady groove, memorable but understated melodic motif.

Background score for a narrated video about creativity, technology and digital avatars. Spacious arrangement that leaves room for spoken narration. Short intro, gentle progression, consistent energy and a clean ending. No vocals, no humming, no vocal chops, no dramatic drops. Original melody.

Este prompt documenta la dirección musical del proyecto. Se puede adaptar para crear una banda sonora propia; cada generación produce resultados distintos.
